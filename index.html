How to reach 7–8

For the highest MYP Criterion A achievement, practise more than recalling facts:

i. Explain scientific knowledge accurately and in detail.

ii. Apply knowledge to solve problems in familiar and unfamiliar situations.

iii. Analyse and evaluate information to make scientifically supported judgements.

These are revision aims, not an official grading decision. A teacher must apply the school's current MYP 1 rubric to actual assessment evidence.

Command term toolkit

State / Identify: short, precise fact.

Describe: give detailed characteristics.

Explain: give reasons and scientific mechanisms.

Compare: similarities and differences.

Apply / Predict: use knowledge in a scenario.

Analyse: interpret evidence and relationships.

Evaluate: weigh evidence and reach a justified conclusion.

Source and coverage

Based on the photographed pages of Sciences Department, Y6, MYP 1, 2026–27, Unit 1: Living Things. Body Systems, including pages 67, 68, 70, 76 and 82, plus adjacent photographed pages. Topics include biomolecules, cells, classification, vital functions, nutrition, digestion, absorption and reproduction. Some intervening pages were not provided, so this is not yet a complete unit question bank.

Model answers add standard scientific clarifications where necessary. For example, the statement that the body replaces all its cells every seven years is an oversimplification and should not be memorised as a scientific fact.
